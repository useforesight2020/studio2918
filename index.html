Studio 2918 LLC is a small software studio founded by an optometrist in Oklahoma. We build tools that take busywork off the plates of doctors, staff, and families.

Foresight Live

Workforce and operations for independent practices: staff, schedules, time cards, and who can cover what, built mobile-first for the people who keep a clinic running.

EyeScribe In development

A charting assistant for eye care that learns how each doctor charts, asks when it isn't sure, and handles the clicks so the doctor can focus on the patient. Currently in a pilot at one practice.

Hamm Personal

A household money helper that sorts spending into the right categories, fills in who checks and payment-app transfers went to, and matches item-level receipts.
