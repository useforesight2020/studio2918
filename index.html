Foresight Live

Workforce and operations for independent practices.

Your practice software tracks patients. Foresight tracks the people who take care of them. It gives owners and managers one place to see who's working, who's out, and who can cover what, built mobile-first for the way clinic teams really work.

  • Staff and roles: profiles, skills, and cross-training in one place
  • Time clock and time cards: clock in from a phone; review and approve hours
  • Clinic hub: who's in, who's off, and what needs attention today
  • Coverage: know who can step in when someone calls out

EyeScribe In development

A charting assistant that learns how you chart.

Most AI scribes write a generic note. EyeScribe watches how each doctor actually charts in their own EHR (which findings go where, how they grade, how they plan) and learns to do it the same way. When it isn't sure, it asks instead of guessing.

  • Learns your style: your grades, your categories, your plans and recalls
  • Hands-free: listens during the exam and handles the clicks
  • Asks, doesn't guess: anything unfamiliar comes back to the doctor
  • Doctor stays in charge: every chart is reviewed and signed by the doctor

Currently in a pilot at one independent practice.

Hamm Personal

A household money helper that does the tedious part.

Hamm takes the busywork out of keeping a household budget. It sorts spending into the right categories, figures out who checks and payment-app transfers went to, and matches item-level receipts so you can answer questions like "how much did we spend on diapers this year?"

  • Auto-sorting: card charges filed into the right budget categories
  • Lookups: payees for checks, Venmo, Zelle, and Cash App
  • Receipts: item-level detail from everyday stores
